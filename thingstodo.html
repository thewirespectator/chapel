Things to Do in Chapel-en-le-Frith

Discover historic streets, countryside walks, family activities and places to explore in Chapel-en-le-Frith and the surrounding High Peak.

Explore our town

Known historically as the Capital of the Peak, Chapel-en-le-Frith combines a characterful old town with access to the beautiful countryside of the Peak District. Whether you live locally or are visiting for the day, here are some ideas to get you started.

🏛️

Explore the Old Town

Wander around the historic Market Place, see the traditional stocks and ancient cross, and explore the old streets around Church Brow and St Thomas Becket Church.

Discover local history ↗
🥾

Walk up Eccles Pike

Head into the countryside for a rewarding walk up Eccles Pike. The summit offers panoramic views across the High Peak on a clear day.

Walking and visitor information ↗
🌳

Visit Memorial Park

Enjoy some time outdoors at Memorial Park, which has green space, a children's playground, skate and BMX ramps, a bowling green and multi-use sports facilities.

More local activities ↗
🌊

Discover Combs Reservoir

Visit the scenic reservoir near Chapel and enjoy views of the surrounding hills. It is also associated with local sailing activities.

Find a local walking route

Explore the parish using published walking routes, including the Bradshaw Hall, Eccles House and Five Halls walks. Check route length and conditions before setting off.

Planning your visit?

Check the official visitor information and walking guides before travelling. Outdoor routes can include uneven paths, hills and muddy sections, so choose a walk that suits your ability and check local conditions.
